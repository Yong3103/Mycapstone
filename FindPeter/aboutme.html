About my story

In Find Peter, you come home and discover that your dog is missing. You search the house, backyard, street, and small park.

Peter chased a squirrel through the gap by the open gate. He followed it to the park and stayed under a tree.

The theme: Careful observation and searching can help solve problems.

People in the story

Character Role
You Peter's owner, searching for clues
Parent Helps you and brings a leash
Neighbor Saw Peter chasing a squirrel

My story plan

  1. Discover the problem
    • Peter is not in the house.
    • The backyard gate is open.
  2. Search for clues
    • Follow the paw prints.
    • Ask your parent and neighbor.
  3. Bring Peter home
    • Find him at the park.
    • Close the gate and block the gap.
